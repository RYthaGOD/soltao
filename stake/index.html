Stake your Solana TAO, in a wallet you own Bridge your TAO back to Solana

One Solana transaction. Your TAO goes home over the LayerZero bridge it came in on, and this page stakes it to the validator you choose, owned by your own Bittensor wallet. There is no receipt token and no soltao contract: every key along the way comes from your signature, and nobody else holds one. Send free TAO from the Bittensor wallet your signature creates back to your Solana wallet as canonical TAO, over the same LayerZero bridge. The page signs every Bittensor step with keys only you hold. This moves free TAO: to bring back staked TAO, unstake it first from "Show what this Bittensor wallet holds" in step 2.

Run by the maintainer of the Solana TAO Board, who also launched the SOLTAO coin (see the disclosure below). Your keys are made and kept in this browser and never sent to them, and there is no soltao contract holding funds. You do trust this page's code, which is open source. Its only charge is a flat SOL fee, shown in step 4 before you sign.

      The LayerZero fee includes 0.001 TAO dropped on your transit account to pay for its first Bittensor transaction; what it does not use comes back to you. The Solana transaction fee is added by your wallet. The transaction is simulated against mainnet before your wallet asks you to sign.

      Your Bittensor wallet funds your transit account, which wraps the TAO and sends it over LayerZero; the LayerZero fee and gas are paid in TAO there. Gas it does not use stays on the transit account, and this page returns it to your Bittensor wallet next time you sign in. If your Solana wallet has no TAO account yet, the bridge's delivery carries the rent to open one. No soltao fee is charged for bridging back.

      Keep this page open until step 5 finishes. Once the bridge delivers, usually within minutes, the page signs the Bittensor steps with your transit key; your wallet will not ask again. If the page closes, come back, sign the same message in step 2, and it finishes.

    ?What you are trusting

    Every hop, and who holds a key over it

    Canonical Solana TAO

    A LayerZero OFT listed by Sunrise. Its Solana program is upgradeable, and the upgrade key and the OFT admin key are the same single key (read on chain, 21 Sep 2026).

    wTAO on Bittensor EVM

    The OFT's other end, holding the TAO that backs Solana's supply. Not upgradeable; its bridge settings are held by a 3-of-4 Safe.

    Your transit account

    Not a contract: a Bittensor EVM account whose key comes from your signature, like your wallet does. It only talks to wTAO and Bittensor's own staking and transfer precompiles, and it only ever sends to your coldkey. If a step fails, your TAO waits there until you sign again. Source ↗

    This page

    It builds the transaction, derives your keys and runs the Bittensor steps in your browser. One self-hosted script, no third-party code loaded at runtime. It stores no keys; an unfinished route's settings stay in this browser until it finishes.

    Fees: soltao charges a flat SOL fee on every route, shown in step 4 before you sign and paid in the same transaction. LayerZero's network fee is quoted live from the TAO program itself. Bittensor gas is paid in TAO, out of what arrives. There is no soltao contract to charge anything else.
