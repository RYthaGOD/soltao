Someone moved 43.05 TAO through soltao.xyz/stake today, about $13,200. The fee was 0.280 SOL, the full 0.25%.

$SOLTAO is not a coupon, and you do not need it. 10 million in the wallet you connect takes the fee to 0.1875%. 50 million takes it to 0.125%. On today's send, half off would have saved 0.14 SOL. Under about 0.5 TAO the 0.0035 SOL minimum still applies, so a small move pays the same either way.

The coin also takes 1% of every transfer. That accrues in TAO and is split across holders when the pot is worth sending. There is no schedule, and it is separate from the route fee.

The cut is for someone who bridges at this size and comes back. It matters more if staking a subnet feels like trading its alpha.
