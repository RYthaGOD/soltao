The Worst Issues

1. Live buttons without live money

What’s wrong: Unstake, Move, Claim, subnet stake through this page, and first-time ATA arrival are on for everyone. Only a root forward stake and a free-TAO return to an existing token account have actually landed.

Why it matters: The next real user is the pilot. If Move or a fresh ATA return reverts in a way the page mishandles, they lose trust and maybe gas, and you find out in production.

What good looks like: One Craig-approved run each, then the honesty line can shrink because it is no longer true.

2. Access is not acquisition

What’s wrong: The page is a complete corridor. There is still no named Solana audience, no subnet promoter, no ten people who already hold taoC…BRrBY and asked for this.

Why it matters: 0.25% of zero is zero. Engineering cannot create the holders.

What good looks like: A list of ten people or one subnet that will send a cohort through ?netuid= this month. Until then, do not call this a growth product.

3. The name is doing three jobs

What’s wrong: Board, gateway, and a StonkFun coin share “soltao”. The disclosure is honest. The first impression is still “is this the coin?”

Why it matters: Partners and cautious holders bounce on conflict, not on the 0.25% fee.

What good looks like: One spoken sentence: “soltao.xyz stakes the canonical Solana TAO mint; the SOLTAO coin is unrelated and optional.” The header almost says it. The URL still does not.

4. SIWS is the entire custody model

What’s wrong: One domain-bound signature is the Bittensor wallet. That is elegant and it is a loaded gun. Phishing soltao.xyz, or a third-party script, is catastrophic.

Why it matters: There is no contract to pause. There is no admin key. There is only CSP and the user’s brain.

What good looks like: Keep script-src 'self'. Never add analytics. The warning copy stays. Hardware-wallet non-determinism stays disclosed.

5. Core loop is a filing cabinet

What’s wrong: Holdings are excellent as a lookup. They are not a reason to open the tab on Tuesday. Alpha compounds on-chain whether you look or not.

Why it matters: Utility products without a return trigger get one session and a bookmark that dies.

What good looks like: A shareable subnet page someone actually sends, plus a last-look delta people check after emissions. Do not fake notifications you cannot send without their keys.

Common Sins Detected

  • Phantom users: Test wallets and one operator’s return are not traction. Do not report “wallets connected.”
  • Bridge to nowhere (partial): The tech has a user in mind. Distribution is still a grant-shaped hole: capable corridor, unpaid traffic.
  • Jargon overload (partial): Hero is plain. Step 3 still says Alpha, take, netuid. Info toggles help; they do not disappear the words.
  • Token-first: Avoided for the route. The listed SOLTAO coin is the remaining smell. Keep it marked ours and never required.
  • Wallet gate (partial): Value is visible before connect. Doing anything still requires a wallet in step 1. Correct for a signer product; still a bounce for browsers.

UX Red Flags

  • Wallet before value: Softened. Hero and hops show the trip. Connect is still the first action.
  • Simulation / fees: Present. Review quotes LayerZero, priority, 0.25%, USD. This is not the failure mode.
  • Error recovery: Resume and “do not send again” exist. Uncertain confirm is correct and anxiety-inducing. Keep it.
  • Mobile: Deep links exist. A newcomer completing the full phone journey has not been evidenced.
  • Shareable state: ?netuid= and ?hotkey= work. That is the growth surface. It is unused if no one posts the link.
  • Token without USD: Review has a dollars row. Holdings worth is in TAO with an optional USD total. Acceptable.

Fix These Now

  1. Highest impact: One real-funds unstake (and, if it frees enough, return) plus one subnet stake through the live origin. Until those land, the product’s honest sentence is still “the doorway works; the back rooms are simulated.”
  2. Easiest win: Give one subnet a ?netuid= link and a human who will post it. Copy is already the way in and the way back. The missing piece is a URL in someone else’s channel.
  3. Existential: Name ten people who hold canonical Solana TAO and want it staked. If you cannot, stop adding features. The corridor is built. The market is the open question.
