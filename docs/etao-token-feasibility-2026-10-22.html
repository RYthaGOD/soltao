A Solana token backed by Bittensor stake

Feasibility assessment · 22 October 2026 · proposed architecture, not a deployed product

Feasible in principle: a Solana receipt token can represent redeemable shares of a Bittensor staking vault. eTAO changes emission allocation; it does not automatically create this token or move staking execution to Solana.

Candidate lifecycle

Solana TAO deposit → confirmed bridge delivery → controlled Bittensor staking vault → verified share issuance → transferable Solana receipt. Redemption reserves or burns the shares exactly once, unwinds the backing and returns TAO to Solana, with pending and recovery states.

A root-only proof of concept would isolate share and bridge accounting from subnet alpha allocation decisions. It still has variable returns, fees and cross-chain risks. A subnet strategy would add alpha price risk and changing eTAO allocations. Neither has a guaranteed exchange rate or yield.
