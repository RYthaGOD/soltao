Buying it, and the thing people get wrong afterwards

  1. Solana wallet

    Fund SOL

    You need SOL in a Solana wallet — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack. SOL is the gas and the deepest route in. Keep a little spare: a first-time TAO buy opens a token account, and that account has to be funded to stay rent-exempt.

    Token-account rent: ~0.0015 SOL, returned if you close it

  2. Jupiter

    Swap, with slippage set by hand

    Open the prefilled route, then confirm the output mint matches the address above character for character — aggregator search will happily show you five things called TAO. Read the price-impact line before signing: the book is thin, so a size that is nothing in SOL is a real haircut here.

    Depth check: read the impact line, not the spot price

  3. What you hold

    Know what you are holding

    A wrapped claim on TAO, tradeable on Solana, kept near the native price by arbitrage. It does not stake, it does not earn emissions, and it will not show up on Taostats.

    Subnet emissions on this token: none

The frictionone chain short

The door home does not open where you need it

Withdrawing on Sunrise burns the SPL token and delivers wTAO on Bittensor EVM, where this wrapper was issued from: a wrapped ERC-20 you still have to unwrap, at an H160 address rather than the SS58 coldkey that subnet staking runs on. A plain wallet transfer cannot reach an SS58 address. The last hop is btcli evm send-to-ss58 through the balance-transfer precompile, or a deposit to your coldkey's EVM mirror that you then claim. It is a terminal, not a wallet click, and nothing on the Solana side tells you it is coming.

Two structural details before you size a position. The token is a LayerZero OFT carrying 6 shared decimals against the mint's 9, so every cross-chain amount is rounded down to 0.000001 TAO and the remainder does not travel. And the OFT store names a single admin key, with a fee parameter and a pause flag in the same account — read on 20 Sep 2026, the fee is 0 and transfers are not paused. That is not an accusation. It is a structure, and it sits between you and your TAO.

Route and origin chain from Sunrise's listed assets — TAO, Bittensor EVM, LayerZero, listed 5 May 2026 — and withdrawal timing, rate limits and queuing from its withdraw guide. The OFT store 8vJKzz…jZdDg, its LayerZero V2 endpoint 76y77p…gjEn6, the 6 shared decimals and the unpaused zero-fee state were read from the mint account on 20 Sep 2026. The last hop is Bittensor's own EVM money-flow guide.

The path that isn't thisno wrapper

You can skip the wrapper entirely

Allways — Bittensor subnet 7, settled on Solana — swaps native assets with no wrapped token anywhere in the path: miners post collateral, validators verify both legs on-chain, the contract slashes on failure. Its repository lists SOL and TAO as hubs with SOL ↔ TAO among the live pairs, which is the thing the wrapper cannot do: SOL in your wallet becomes native TAO in your Bittensor wallet. It is beta software and says so plainly.

If you land on Bittensor EVM anyway, you can stake from there without going further. The V2 staking precompile takes a netuid, and addStakeLimit carries a limit price and a partial-fill flag, so you are not staking blind into a moving alpha pool. 

addStake(bytes32 hotkey, uint256 amount, uint256 netuid)
addStakeLimit(bytes32 hotkey, uint256 amount,
  uint256 limit_price, bool allow_partial, uint256 netuid)

Pairs and trust model from the Allways repository, read 20 Sep 2026 — "use at your own risk. No warranty." Signatures copied from Opentensor's evm-bittensor interface; the 0x…0805 precompile answers on mainnet (read 21 Sep 2026). Confirm Allways is live before you rely on it. And know that Bittensor EVM is close to empty: about $27K of DeFi across one DEX against $495M on the base chain (DefiLlama, 20 Sep 2026).

The route this site runs

soltao's stake route makes that trip in one Solana transaction: your TAO goes home over the same LayerZero bridge and is staked to the validator you pick, owned by a Bittensor wallet you hold. No receipt token and no soltao contract: every key along the way comes from your own signature. soltao charges a flat SOL fee on it, shown before you sign.

Doors that are not yours

Ethereum's wTAO is a different bridge, and it is not on the ladder above: closed-source, one pseudonymous operator, withdrawals verified by hand at a cadence the operator described as "at least once a day morning/night", holding about $28M of TAO. Real money, real structure — but routing a Solana position home through it would add a manual custodian you do not otherwise touch. Tensorplex is closing: Tensorplex Stake and the tTAO bridge are deprecated, with manual withdrawals only during the wind-down. Operator and quote: DL News, February 2024, when the bridge held $82M; the $28M is DefiLlama's on 20 Sep 2026 and is not refreshed here. Deprecation notice read at stake.tensorplex.ai, 20 Sep 2026.
